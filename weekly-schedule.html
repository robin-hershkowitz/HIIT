weekly plan · level 1

Four sessions, not three hours a day.

You don't drill every skill every day — you rotate, and you let skills overlap. One on-skates day, one footwork day, one strength day, one flex day. Around two hours a week, and every assessment skill gets touched. Skating-only skills get priority on skate days because nothing off-skates fully replaces them.

Skills overlap

One timer hits four or five assessment skills at once. Crossovers, scissoring, and getting low share the same hips and edges. You're further along than the list looks.

Rest is training

Rotating means some skills rest while others work. That rest is when the left-leg neuromuscular work consolidates. Resting a skill isn't neglecting it.

Protect skate time

Crossovers, stops, glides, backward skating need skates. Those get priority when you're on wheels. Strength and footwork flex around them — even in socks.

Spend time on the gap

Front-load what you'll be tested on and are furthest from passing: left-leg work, back-leg crossover push, the stops. Don't polish what's already solid.

Day 1 — Skate on skates · lane
The skills only skating builds. Priority day — nothing off-skates replaces these.
Skills: crossovers (back-leg push), T-stops & plow stops, pivot transitions, one-foot glides, backward skating, scissoring.
Use: Skating Lane Drills · Skating Lane 2 (With Hops)
~30 min · one lane timer
Day 2 — Footwork off skates · floor
Agility and balance off the wheels — trains the footwork patterns that transfer straight to skates.
Skills: grapevines (both directions), side shuffles, quick step / happy feet, 30-sec single-leg balance (left bias), lateral lunges.
Use: Grapevine & Agility HIIT · Level 1 Strength & Balance
~30 min · one footwork timer
Day 3 — Strength off skates · weights
Build the muscles that power the skating — hips and posterior chain for stops and crossovers, upper body for posture and balance.
Skills: hip & adductor strength (plow stops), explosive hip extension (jammer starts), back & shoulders, left-leg loading.
Use: Plow + Jump HIIT · Upper Body Weights HIIT · Cardio & Core
~30 min · one strength timer
Day 4 — Flex your call
Whatever felt weakest this week — or genuine rest. Both are legitimate. Listen to the ankle.
Options: repeat the weakest skill from days 1–3, do a light cardio timer, stretch and mobility only, or take the day fully off.
Use: Pure Cardio HIIT · Derby.Fit · or rest
~0–30 min · optional

The one thing to let go of

You don't need to be even across everything every week. Some weeks the skate day matters most; some weeks it's the left-leg work. Pick the rotation, protect the skate sessions, and trust that overlap and rest are doing work even on the days a skill sits idle. Sharp pain or a sore ankle the next morning means scale back — you lose weeks pushing through the wrong pain, almost nothing by resting a day.

A rhythm, not a rulebook. Move days around to fit your week.
